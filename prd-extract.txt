FarmLink Nigeria Comprehensive Product
 Requirements Document (PRD)
Executive Summary
FarmLink Nigeria is a digital marketplace designed to connect farmers, livestock producers, and
food vendors directly with buyers across Nigeria.
The platform allows agricultural producers to list products while buyers can discover, compare, and
purchase fresh produce safely.
The system includes secure payment infrastructure, seller verification, buyer reviews, and delivery
confirmation. Payments are held in escrow and released only when buyers confirm successful
delivery. This model builds trust in online agricultural commerce.
FarmLink aims to solve a major inefficiency in Nigeria's agricultural supply chain: farmers lack direct
access to markets while buyers lack trusted sources of fresh farm produce.
Problem Statement
Nigeria's agricultural sector faces several structural problems:
1. Limited Market Access for Farmers
Many farmers rely on middlemen who reduce their profits and control pricing.
2. Lack of Trust in Online Food Purchases
Buyers are hesitant to purchase agricultural products online due to fraud risks and unreliable
delivery.
3. Fragmented Marketplaces
Most farm products are currently sold through informal channels such as roadside markets or social
media platforms.
4. Lack of Price Transparency
Farmers and buyers often lack accurate data on market pricing, leading to underpricing or
exploitation.


Proposed Solution
FarmLink Nigeria provides a centralized online marketplace where farmers and food vendors can
list agricultural products and buyers can purchase them securely.
Key elements of the solution include:
 Verified farmer profiles
 Product listing and search functionality
 Secure escrow payment system
 Buyer review and rating system
 Location-based product discovery
 Order tracking and dispute resolution
The platform creates a structured digital market for agricultural commerce.

Future Features


AI Price Insights
AI-powered tools will provide farmers with market price insights based on demand trends and
location data. The AI suggests price ranges but does not enforce pricing.
Bulk Marketplace
Restaurants and large buyers can post bulk purchase requests and farmers can submit supply
offers.
Farm Verification Program
Physical or documentation-based farm verification to increase buyer trust.
Harvest Alerts
Farmers can announce newly harvested produce to attract buyers quickly.
Farm Map
Interactive map showing farms and products near buyers.
Revenue Model
FarmLink will generate revenue through:
Transaction Commission
The platform charges a 5 percent commission on every completed order.
Seller Advertising
Sellers can promote their products using banner advertisements and featured listings.
Vendor Subscription Plans
Optional subscription tiers that provide advanced analytics, product promotions, and marketplace
visibility.
Featured Listings
Sellers can pay to promote products in top search positions.


Advertisement System
The platform supports sponsored advertisements in multiple locations:
Homepage Banner Ads
Large promotional banners shown on the homepage.
Sidebar Advertisements
Ads displayed on product and browsing pages.
Featured Product Promotions
Highlighted listings placed at the top of product search results.
Sellers apply through their dashboard and advertisements are approved by administrators before
being published.
